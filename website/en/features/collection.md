# Collection

"Collection" in the sidebar adds up how much you have played, and has two shelves: games you finished, and games you put away.

## The three numbers at the top

- **Play time**: all games on all devices together, see [Play time](./playtime.md).
- **Games**: how many games there are. The versions of one game count once.
- **Achievements**: how many are unlocked. Only games whose achievements this PC knows count; the "?" has the numbers, see [Achievements](./achievements.md).

## Finished

On the game's page, click "…" and choose "Mark finished" to put it on this shelf; "Not finished" takes it off again. The game page's status shows a "Finished" mark.

## Archived

When you've finished a game and want to uninstall it, but keep its saves, play time and achievements: on its page, click "…" and choose "Archive".

- Archived games are no longer shown in the library, only on this shelf of the collection. A line at the bottom of the library, "N archived", leads here.
- Saves, play time and achievements are in the cloud; archiving deletes nothing. You can uninstall the game on this PC.
- To play again, open it from the collection and choose "Unarchive": it's back in the library. Once the game is installed again, add it to this PC as described in [Multiple devices](../sync/devices.md), and its saves come down.

## The same on every device

The shelves are kept on the game's record in the cloud, so what you mark on one device shows on the others. The versions of one game (the Steam version, an emulator's…) go on and off a shelf together.

A game that hasn't uploaded any saves has no record in the cloud yet and can't go on a shelf; sync it once first.
