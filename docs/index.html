Ludonimbus

给 Steam 里的「非 Steam 游戏」补上云存档：模拟器、重制版、第三方游戏。

Ludonimbus brings Steam-Cloud-like save sync to non-Steam shortcuts (emulators, recompilations, third-party games). Saves are pulled before a game starts and uploaded after it exits, with per-device version history and conflict handling. Storage is your own Google Drive, NAS / WebDAV server, S3-compatible bucket or any folder you choose.

功能 / Features

像 Steam 云存档一样

从 Steam 启动时先拉取云端存档，退出游戏后自动上传。
Pull before launch, upload after exit.

删游戏不丢存档

重装游戏、换文件夹、换电脑后，第一次启动就自动恢复。
Saves come back after reinstalling or moving a game.

版本历史

每个版本记录时间和设备名，可以恢复、导出、锁定。
Per-device version history with restore and export.

冲突时两份都留

两台设备都改过存档时让你选择，没选的那份也会保留。
Conflicts keep both sides.

添加游戏不用找存档

扫描本机的非 Steam 游戏、Epic / GOG / Xbox 游戏、模拟器和已有的存档，勾选后批量添加。
Scan this PC for games and existing saves, then add them in bulk.

一键加入 Steam

不在 Steam 里的游戏一键加入，自动开启云存档，从 SteamGridDB 挑选封面。
Add games to Steam in one click, with artwork from SteamGridDB.

你自己的云盘

存档放在你自己的 Google Drive、NAS、WebDAV / S3 存储或任意文件夹里，开发者没有服务器。
Your own Google Drive, NAS, WebDAV / S3 storage or folder; no developer servers.

开源

Open source (MIT), built on Ludusavi's save database.
